## ⚠️ THE DAY 1 TRAP — read once, then never again

The trap is not refusing to measure. Nobody refuses on Day 1.

The trap is **THE ELOQUENT CONFESSION** — vagueness upgraded to poetry. You will feel the pull to write something deep and true-sounding about your situation instead of writing the number. Insight about your finances is not a balance. A beautiful paragraph about chaos is still chaos, now with better lighting.

The tell: **adjectives where numbers should be.** "Heavy." "Complicated." "Roughly." "I think around…" Every approximation for a number you could look up in ninety seconds is the trap firing. The worst field on the sheet — the one you already roughly know — will try to stay approximate all day. That field is the whole day.

The moment you catch yourself writing *about* the sheet instead of *into* it — stop. That is the leak, caught live. Log it and return to the field.
