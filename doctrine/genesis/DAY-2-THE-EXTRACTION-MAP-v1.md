## ⚠️ THE DAY 2 TRAP — read once, then never again

The trap is not missing extractions. You'll find plenty.

The trap is **THE MISSING ROW.** The map will fill with subscriptions, apps, and acquaintances — everything except the one access you are afraid to name. The most extractive system in your life is the one that does not appear on today's map. Extraction mapping without that row is a grievance inventory: resentment feels like clarity, and revokes nothing.

Second head of the same trap: **moral accounting.** Cataloguing who takes from you can become the day's product. It isn't. The day's product is one revoked access with evidence. A map of thieves changes nothing; a changed lock does.

The audit at day's end is not "is the map full?" It is: **who is absent from it?** The moment you notice yourself steering around a name — that's the leak, caught live. Log it and write the row.
