## ⚠️ THE DAY 3 TRAP — read once, then never again

The trap is **THE HUMBLE DISCOUNT** — "that doesn't really count" wearing the face of honesty.

You will not resist today because wins are hard to remember. You'll resist because every entry in the Force Ledger is a receipt against your excuses. Proven capacity is billable. The moment a capability enters the record with a witness, "I can't" dies and "I won't" is all that's left standing — and "I won't" has to answer questions that "I can't" never did. So SELF discounts: *anyone could have done that · it was luck · the conditions were easy · it doesn't count because it wasn't finished/paid/public.* Discounting keeps SELF in "potential" status, and potential owes nothing.

The trap has a second head: **THE INFLATED LEDGER** — padding the record with claims no witness can confirm. Both heads are the same move: keeping the record unusable. The WITNESS column kills both. An entry is either witnessed or marked UNWITNESSED — and an unwitnessed entry can't be discounted *or* inflated, only tested later.

Catch the discount live: the words "doesn't really count" are a tally, not a judgment.
