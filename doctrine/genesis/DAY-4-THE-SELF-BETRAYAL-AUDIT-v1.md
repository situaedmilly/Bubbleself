## ⚠️ THE DAY 4 TRAP — read once, then never again

The trap is **THE SHAME PAYMENT.**

Today's audit will hurt, and the hurt will offer to settle the account. Feeling bad presents itself as accountability — it hurts enough to feel like justice and costs nothing structural. Shame is how SELF pays itself off: guilt discharges the debt emotionally, so the repair never has to execute. A node that ends today feeling terrible and repairing nothing has bought relief with pain and called it integrity.

Second head: **THE CONFESSION LOOP.** Confessing the same betrayal eloquently for the nth time is not an audit — at n≥2, the confession IS the betrayal, renewed. You've said these words before. Today's question is not whether you can say them again. It's whether the artifact exists by this time tomorrow.

The audit accepts exactly one currency: **repair executed.** Feelings are not legal tender here. The moment guilt arrives and offers to settle — tally it, timestamp it, and return to the artifact.
