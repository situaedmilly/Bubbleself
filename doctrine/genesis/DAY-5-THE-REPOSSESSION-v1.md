You will want to build the allocation system — new categories, new labels, a cleaner app, color-coded calendar — with total sincerity, and schedule the actual transfer for "when things settle." That is reorganizing, not repossessing. Motion inside the map; nothing moved in the territory.

The moment you catch yourself perfecting the structure instead of executing the transfer — that's D-002 wearing a ledger. Tally it, timestamp it, move the resource.
