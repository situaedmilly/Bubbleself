## 0. Lineage — spec for spec, third eye for third eye

The Genesis Protocol is not written from theory. It is ported from two field
instruments that were actually run — **DAY 3 · THE RECEIPTS** and **DAY 4 · THE DAY
AFTER** of the 14-day trial — and from the Genesis manifesto (the seven-day arc:
Raw State → Extraction Map → Proof of Force → Self-Betrayal Audit → Repossession →
Reality Mutation → Evidence Court).

The port is literal. Every mechanic that made the field instruments falsifiable is
carried into every Genesis day, and every Genesis day receives its own **third eye**
— a named, day-specific trap, the way Day 3 named "converting the protocol into
another artifact" and Day 4 named "the victory lap."

| Source mechanic (field instruments) | Genesis port |
|---|---|
| THE GATE — Ship-One artifact declared before the day starts | Every day opens with a Gate. No named artifact = the day does not start. |
| Locked artifact + Definition of Done + two-option completion statement | Every Gate carries a DoD. Completion statements have exactly two options. No third option. |
| The named Trap ("read once, then never again") | Every day carries its own Trap — the day's specific escape route, named before it fires. |
| OPENING DEBT — unsettled prior evidence blocks the day | Days 2–7 settle yesterday first. Ambiguous yesterdays are banned; written failure unlocks, vagueness does not. |
| MORNING COMMAND — before phone, read aloud, don't soften | Daily, day-specific. |
| Live-log stations — "log it the instant you touch it," feeling-not-reason columns | Every station that observes behavior logs at the moment of contact. The last column is always a feeling, never a reason. |
| Timestamped tallies (leak tally + named-lie tally) | Daily general leak tally + a day-specific lie tally with timestamps. The timestamp is the tell. |
| THE MIRROR QUESTION — yesterday's words returned verbatim | The runtime returns SELF's own prior words, side by side with today's behavior. |

| Streak logic — a win is a down payment, not a finish line | Days 2–7 carry the running count: sealed / failed-measured. |
| Discomfort score (0–10, under 7 = the easy version) | Daily, with a day-specific threshold line. |

## 1. The chamber

The first seven days cannot feel like a premium journal wrapped in violet fog. That
would be aesthetic self-help. Genesis Nodes are not entering to "discover
themselves." They already know they are dangerous when focused. They have watched
themselves earn, improvise, survive pressure, build around broken infrastructure,
flip money, carry households, create leverage — and still under-command their own
reality.

The initiation confronts a more severe question:

> **Why does a capable SELF continue producing a reality beneath its demonstrated
> capacity?**

That is the chamber. Two properties are non-negotiable, and every instrument in this
protocol is built to enforce them:

- **DISRUPTIVE** — every day can fail. A protocol that cannot fail cannot measure.
- **MANIFEST** — every day must change matter. Internal shift without external state
  change does not seal.

This is not a seven-day challenge. It is a controlled seizure of executive
authority. The participant does not leave feeling understood. They leave with a
falsified excuse, a named extraction loop, a proven capability record, an authority
boundary, a live command, a material mutation, and evidence that their reality is no
longer running untouched.

## 2. The Genesis Node

A Genesis Node is not simply "like-minded." Like-minded is too weak — people can
think similarly and remain mutually stagnant.

A Genesis Node is: **a person already producing disproportionate motion inside an
environment that does not yet recognize, organize, or properly capitalize that
motion.**

They may be clocked into somebody else's machine while building their own after
work; paying thousands in obligations while still accumulating capital; learning
trading, software, content, systems, logistics, sales, or operations in public;
treated as "potential" despite producing evidence; surrounded by people who watched
the shift happen in real time; no longer psychologically available for a small life;
still leaking power through chaos, reaction, overextension, debt, impulse, divided
attention, or ungoverned ambition.

These are not wounded consumers. They are **under-institutionalized builders**.
Their issue is not inspiration. Their issue is that their executive capacity has not
yet been constitutionalized.

## 4. Shared instrumentation (binding on every day document)

1. **THE GATE.** Answered before anything else. One line, one artifact, declared
   before the day starts. Allowed: nouns that will verifiably exist by sundown.
   Banned everywhere, all seven days: *work on, think about, improve, research,
   design, plan, get a sense of, figure out.* No artifact named = the day does not
   start. Every Gate carries a Definition of Done and a two-option completion
   statement ("I shipped X." / "I did not ship X."). No third option.

2. **THE TRAP.** Read once, then never again. Each day's trap is that day's escape
   route named in advance. The moment SELF catches itself inside the trap — running
   the trap *is* the leak, caught live. Log it and return to the Gate artifact.
   Refining any protocol document instead of executing it is always the trap's
   first form (D-002).

3. **OPENING DEBT.** Days 2–7 open by settling yesterday. A sealed yesterday or a
   written failure line ("No artifact produced") unlocks today. An ambiguous
   yesterday blocks the day. Intention is not evidence; the artifact is.

4. **MORNING COMMAND.** Before phone. Before music. 5 minutes. Read aloud. Don't
   soften. Each day's command is written in the day document and may not be edited
   on the day it is run (editing the command is D-002).

5. **STATIONS.** The day's structured work. Behavior stations log at the instant of
   contact. Wherever a station asks why something was abandoned, the answer is a
   **feeling, not a reason** — *confused, uncertain, embarrassed, bored* — never
   "it wasn't efficient."

6. **MIDDAY TALLIES.** Two counters, timestamps mandatory on the second:
   the general leak tally (any escape from the day's work) and the day's named-lie
   tally (Day 1: approximations · Day 2: virtue-words · Day 3: discounts · Day 4:
   shame-hits · Day 5: laters · Day 6: worthiness delays · Day 7: story urges).
   The time-of-day pattern is the data: the lie spikes right after friction and
   right after small wins.

7. **THE MIRROR QUESTION.** The runtime returns SELF's own prior words — verbatim,
   dated — beside today's behavior. No paraphrase. No shame language. Only
   constitutional contradiction: SELF SAID / SELF DID.

8. **THE MATERIAL ACTION.** Every day changes matter outside the participant's
   head: an exposure, a revocation, a conversion, a repair, a transfer, a mutation,
   a sentencing. Evidence standard stated before execution. A day whose only
   changes are internal does not seal.

9. **EVENING CONFRONTATION.** Voice memo, day-specific stem, hard 3:00 floor. When
   the first clean stopping sentence arrives, that is the halfway mark, not the
   end — the truth lives past the point where it is comfortable to stop. Listen
   once. Don't delete.

10. **NARRATIVE COMPLETION CHECK.** Did SELF feel a future win before earning it?
    If yes — what, and at what hour. D-001's tell is the timestamp.

11. **DOCTRINE VERDICT.** From today's data only. Every doctrine states its
    falsification condition in advance, and a falsified doctrine is a **WIN** — a
    good instrument can kill its own theories. The data picks. The vibe does not
    get a vote.

12. **THE SEAL.** Evidence checklist, discomfort score (0–10, with the day's
    threshold line — under 7 means the easy version ran), Stayed/Fled, Most
    Dangerous Lie, Most Valuable Distinction, Final Sentence ("Today I discovered
    that ______"), the day's seal law, signature, time. **The seal can fail.** No
    artifact = no seal. The honest line is not "great awareness" — it is "No
    artifact produced." That does not make the day worthless. It makes it
    accurately measured — which is the one thing SELF claimed it wanted from the
    start.

## 5. Doctrine registry

Three carried doctrines run all seven days; seven day-doctrines run one day each.
Every doctrine is falsifiable, and its falsification condition is written before
the data arrives.

### Carried (tested daily)

**D-001 · NARRATIVE COMPLETION.** SELF experiences the feeling of a completed
future before material completion, and the feeling substitutes for the act.
*Tell:* the win-feeling spikes right after friction and right after small wins.
*Instrument:* the daily Narrative Completion Check with timestamp.

**D-002 · DESIGN SUBSTITUTION.** Under friction, SELF escapes into architecture —
planning, improving, refining the protocol itself. The map replaces the hammer.
*Instrument:* the design-instead-of-do component of the leak tally; the standing
ban on editing protocol documents on the day they run.

**D-003 · AUTHORSHIP.** Claim: SELF completes self-authored structures at a higher
rate than imposed ones. *Falsification:* low completion across BOTH self-authored
and imposed structures → the mechanism is friction-avoidance, not autonomy —
rewrite the doctrine. Less flattering. More true. *Instrument:* the live structure
log (Time / Structure / Self-authored–Imposed / Completed–Abandoned / feeling one
second before abandonment).

### Day doctrines

**D-101 · VAGUENESS AS SHELTER (Day 1).** SELF keeps facts unmeasured because
precision creates obligation. *Prediction:* resistance clusters on exactly the
fields whose numbers SELF already roughly knows. *Falsification:* every field
fills with exact values at equal speed and the approximation tally stays near
zero → vagueness was a data-access problem, not a shelter. Rewrite it.

**D-102 · AVOIDED REVOCATION (Day 2).** Extraction persists not because it is
hidden but because revocation costs a confrontation SELF avoids. *Prediction:*
SELF can name its heaviest extraction before any mapping (the Gate proves this).
*Falsification:* the map genuinely surprises SELF about its heaviest extraction →
the mechanism is visibility, not avoidance. Rewrite it.

**D-103 · DISCOUNTED CAPACITY (Day 3).** SELF underweights verified wins to
preserve "potential" status, because proven capacity is billable and potential
owes nothing. *Prediction:* the CLAIM step (pricing, witness, publishing)
produces more resistance than the RECALL step. *Falsification:* recall is the
hard part and claiming is easy → the mechanism is memory weighting, not
obligation avoidance. Rewrite it.

**D-104 · SHAME SETTLEMENT (Day 4).** SELF uses guilt as currency — feeling bad
discharges the debt so repair never executes. *Prediction:* shame arrives before
or instead of the repair action, and repair gets postponed once shame is felt.
*Falsification:* the repair executes within 24 hours without a shame spike → the
betrayals were scheduling failures, not settlement structures. Rewrite it.

**D-105 · ALLOCATION DRIFT (Day 5).** Resources without a constitutional
destination are allocated by pressure — by whoever or whatever applies force
first. *Prediction:* SELF designs the allocation and defers the transfer.
*Falsification:* the transfer executes same-day alongside the design → the drift
was absence of structure, now corrected; the doctrine survives as environmental,
not psychological.

**D-106 · REVERSIBILITY PREFERENCE (Day 6).** SELF selects changes that can be
undone unseen, because witnessed, irreversible change can fail publicly.
*Prediction:* mutation candidates get privately drafted and publicly withheld;
"it needs to be better first" appears. *Falsification:* the mutation ships
witnessed and hard-to-reverse before evening → the preference is broken or
absent. Record it.

**D-107 · NARRATIVE SENTENCING (Day 7).** SELF converts a mixed evidence record
into a clean story — triumph or indictment — because stories end and records
carry. *Prediction:* pressure to grade all declarations in the same direction.
*Falsification:* the verdict sheet lands mixed, every verdict carries a
citation, and the thirty-day mission is built from PARTIALLY PROVEN items →
sentencing was resisted. Record it.

## 6. The failure law

- Any day can fail. A failed day is **FAILED-MEASURED** — a lawful terminal state,
  recorded in writing, that unlocks the next day.
- A failed day does not reset the protocol. There is no streak-worship: the streak
  counter records sealed and failed-measured days side by side, because the
  measurement is the streak.
- The protocol itself fails only one way: a day that is **narrated instead of
  measured** — no seal, no failure line, vagueness carried overnight. That is the
  only unrecoverable state, and even it is recoverable by writing the failure line
  late and marking it late.
- Accurately measured beats comfortably narrated. Everywhere. Always.

## 7. Runtime duties (what CONSELFCONVO does each day)

The runtime is the interrogator and the clerk. It is not the judge of content.

The runtime MUST:
- refuse to open a day whose Gate line is empty or contains a banned verb;
- refuse a seal whose evidence checklist is incomplete, mechanically — an
  unchecked box is an unchecked box;
- return SELF's own prior words verbatim and dated in every Mirror Question;
- present SELF SAID / SELF DID side-by-sides without shame language — only
  constitutional contradiction;
- log tally timestamps and surface the time-of-day pattern back to the node;
- accept a written failure line as a lawful settlement of a day;
- record a falsified doctrine as a WIN in the node's record.

The runtime MUST NEVER:
- score the truth of a reflection's content — it carries evidence, it does not
  grade souls;
- soften a confrontation, supply a compliment, or complete a node's sentence;
- accept aspiration as evidence, in any field, on any day;
- upgrade its own evidence — a runtime-generated summary is never a witness;
- infer a seal, a feeling, a verdict, or a repair the node did not explicitly
  submit. (This is the doctrine-layer restatement of the Realm's standing rule:
  the Realm proposes, the authority disposes, and nothing is inferred on the
  human's behalf.)
